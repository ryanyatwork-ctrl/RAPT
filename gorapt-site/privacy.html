Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Belleville Systems collects through GoRAPT, RAPT, HostCopy, getrapt.app, hostcopy.gorapt.com, and related services.

Information we collect

We collect account information, contact information, product settings, property and listing details, pricing rules, calendar information, prompts, generated content, subscription details, and support messages that you provide.

We may collect usage, device, log, diagnostic, security, and analytics information, including browser type, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed, actions taken, and error logs.

How we use information

Belleville Systems uses information to operate the services, provide pricing and listing tools, maintain accounts, process subscriptions, provide support, improve product quality, troubleshoot issues, protect against abuse, and send service or administrative communications.

Service providers

We may share information with vendors that help us provide hosting, authentication, analytics, payment processing, email, logging, database, AI, and infrastructure services. These providers are authorized to process information for the services they provide to us.

AI features

If you use AI features, prompts and related property, listing, or host context may be sent to AI service providers to generate responses. Review AI output before publishing it elsewhere.

Retention, security, and choices

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We use safeguards designed to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information by contacting us at contact@gorapt.com.

Children

The services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
