### Post 4 — Reddit r/airbnb_hosts
**Type:** Genuine, community-safe (value first, link soft and at the end)

**Title:** How I stopped missing local events that spike demand (and undercharging on those nights)

> Sharing a workflow that fixed a specific leak for me, in case it helps someone.
>
> My problem wasn't occupancy — it was that I'd price a weekend weeks in advance and then a concert or a conference would get announced nearby and I'd never adjust. Guests booked my peak nights at my Tuesday price. Death by a thousand cuts.
>
> What actually helped:
> 1. **Check event calendars for your area weekly.** Ticketmaster + Eventbrite + your city's event page. Concerts, sports, graduations, conferences all move demand.
> 2. **Set a floor and a ceiling, not one flat rate.** Weekends, holidays, and event nights should have their own multiplier off your base.
> 3. **Look at the shoulder season honestly.** The money you leave on peak nights often comes back as empty calendars in the slow months if you overprice there. It cuts both ways.
>
> I eventually got tired of doing this by hand across my listings and started using a tool called RAPT that pulls the event data and scores demand per date automatically, then suggests a price I approve. Not required — you can do all of this manually with a spreadsheet and an hour a week. But if you want the automated version it's at getrapt.app.
