**Meta description (≤155 chars):** Learn how to price your Airbnb for local events — find demand early, set event-aware rates, and stop leaving money on sold-out weekends.

**Outline:**

- **H1: How to Price Your Airbnb for Local Events**
  - Intro: the "sold-out weekend at the wrong price" problem; promise a repeatable method, not just "charge more."

- **H2: Why flat pricing quietly costs you the most on your best nights**
  - Underpricing peak demand + discounting dead nights; the double loss.

- **H2: The two pricing mistakes hosts make with events**
  - H3: Reacting too late (pricing off booking pace after demand is visible)
  - H3: Overcorrecting (jacking every nearby event, including ones that don't move *your* guest)

- **H2: How to find demand-driving events before your competitors do**
  - H3: Where to look — city calendars, Eventbrite, sports schedules, festivals, conferences
  - H3: Judging event relevance to *your* property (distance, guest type, length of stay)

- **H2: Building an event-aware pricing framework**
  - H3: Base rate → weekend, holiday, event, and seasonality multipliers
  - H3: Making every price explainable so you can trust and adjust it

- **H2: Manual vs. automated event pricing — when each makes sense**
  - Trade-offs; where a forward-looking tool saves time across multiple listings.

- **H2: Forecasting the revenue impact before the season starts**
  - Using a rolling forecast to plan cash flow and spot soft weeks early.

- **H2: Put it on autopilot with RAPT** *(soft commercial close)*
  - How RAPT surfaces upcoming travel drivers, scores relevance to your property, and suggests explainable rates. CTA → getrapt.app. Secondary mention: HostCopy (hostcopy.gorapt.com) for event-tailored listing copy.

- **FAQ (schema-eligible H2s):**
  - H3: How far in advance should I raise prices for an event?
  - H3: Should I raise my minimum-night stay for events too?
  - H3: Do dynamic pricing tools work for small or single-listing hosts?
