RAPT – Really Awesome Pricing Tool

RAPT is an event-aware pricing tool for short-term rental hosts. It scores local demand from real-world events and recommends nightly rates, so you set the right price, for the right guest, at the right time.

What does RAPT do?

  • Smart Calendar – color-coded demand visualization with event overlays.
  • Event Intelligence – aggregates local events from Eventbrite, city calendars, sports schedules and festivals to score demand.
  • Dynamic Pricing Engine – configurable multipliers for weekends, holidays, events and seasonality, with an explanation for every price.
  • AI Listing Optimizer – titles and descriptions tailored to upcoming events and your target guest.
  • Multi-Property Support – manage any number of properties from one dashboard.
  • Revenue Forecasting – 6-month rolling revenue projections.

How is RAPT different from other pricing tools?

Most tools react to past data. RAPT predicts demand from real-world events happening near your property.

How much does RAPT cost?

  • Free – $0 forever, 1 property.
  • Pro – $14/month, up to 10 properties, full event intelligence, AI listing optimizer and revenue forecasting.
  • Advanced – $29/month, unlimited properties and automation features.
