### Post D — Reddit r/AirBnB & r/airbnb_hosts (genuine, community-safe)
**Format: discussion post, value-first, disclosed tool mention**

> **Title:** How much are local events actually moving your nightly rate? (a small experiment)
>
> I got tired of guessing whether "there's an event that weekend" was worth changing my price over, so I spent a month tracking it on one property.
>
> Method: I pulled every event within ~15 miles for 90 days (city calendar, a sports schedule, and the two big venues near me), tagged each weekend as high/medium/low demand, then compared the nights I raised rates against the nights I didn't.
>
> What I found on my place:
> - Big-event weekends I'd historically left at base price were booking within hours — classic sign I was underpriced.
> - A "flat weekend bump" of +20% was too blunt. Some events justified +50%, some events were locals-only and moved nothing.
> - Mid-week during a multi-day conference was where I'd been leaving the most money, because I never think to raise weekday prices.
>
> Rough result over the month: about 22% more revenue on the same number of booked nights, mostly from pricing the *right* dates higher instead of everything higher.
>
> I ended up automating the tracking with a tool called RAPT (getrapt.app) so I'm not rebuilding a spreadsheet every month — full disclosure, but the manual version works too and I'd genuinely recommend just trying the spreadsheet first to see if the pattern holds for your market.
>
> Curious what everyone else is seeing — are events actually moving your rates, or is it more about seasonality where you are?

*Reddit note: lead with the experiment and the takeaway, disclose the tool plainly, invite discussion, don't drop the link cold. Post in a comment reply if a subreddit's rules prohibit links in the body.*

## 3. SEO Blog Outline

**Working title:** Airbnb Dynamic Pricing: How to Stop Leaving Money on the Table (2026 Guide)
**Primary keyword:** `airbnb dynamic pricing`
**Secondary keywords:** short-term rental pricing strategy, how to price your Airbnb, vacation rental pricing tool, seasonal Airbnb pricing
**Search intent:** Informational → commercial investigation. Host knows flat pricing is costing them and is researching how dynamic pricing works and whether a tool is worth it. Goal: educate, then position RAPT as the low-friction "you stay in control" option.
**Target length:** 1,800–2,400 words

**Outline:**

- **H1: Airbnb Dynamic Pricing: How to Stop Leaving Money on the Table**
  - Intro: the cost of a flat nightly rate, stated in dollars (the 15–40% gap). Promise: what dynamic pricing is, how to do it, and when a tool pays for itself.

- **H2: What is dynamic pricing for short-term rentals?**
  - Plain definition; contrast with flat and simple weekday/weekend pricing.
  - The core idea: price should track *demand per night*, not the calendar in the abstract.

- **H2: Why flat pricing quietly costs hosts thousands**
  - The two failure modes: underpricing high-demand nights (instant bookings = left money) and overpricing dead nights (empty calendar).
  - Mini-example with numbers.

- **H2: The 4 demand signals that should move your nightly rate**
  - H3: Seasonality
  - H3: Day of week (and why "+20% on weekends" is too blunt)
  - H3: Local events (concerts, conferences, sports, festivals)
  - H3: Holidays and lead time / booking pace

- **H2: How to build a short-term rental pricing strategy (step by step)**
  - Set a defensible base rate, layer multipliers, pull a 90-day local event calendar, review booking pace, adjust.

- **H2: Manual pricing vs. a dynamic pricing tool: which is right for you?**
  - Honest tradeoffs; when a spreadsheet is fine and when it breaks (multiple properties, events you can't track by hand).

- **H2: How RAPT prices your calendar (and keeps you in control)**
  - Demand scoring, event intelligence, suggested price with reasoning, current-vs-optimized revenue forecast. Emphasize approve-don't-automate.
  - Soft CTA → getrapt.app

- **H2: Frequently asked questions**
  - H3: Will dynamic pricing hurt my occupancy?
  - H3: How often should I change my Airbnb prices?
  - H3: Does Airbnb's built-in Smart Pricing do this? (short answer: not at this granularity)
  - H3: How much more revenue can I realistically expect?

- **Closing CTA:** Recap the gap; invite reader to see their own calendar's demand map on RAPT.
