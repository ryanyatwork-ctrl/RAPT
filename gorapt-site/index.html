Traffic intent

Different pages for different searches.

Learning about RAPT

Send brand, comparison, privacy, support, and company questions to gorapt.com so visitors can verify who operates the tools.

Trying the product

Send pricing, calendar, forecast, and account intent to getrapt.app where the user can sign in or start the workflow.

Writing listing copy

Send copy-generation intent to HostCopy, with direct language about what happens to submitted property details.

Built to be easy to verify.

GoRAPT keeps product, legal, and support information visible. The site is configured for HTTPS, strong browser security headers, and straightforward contact paths. HostCopy should stay focused and private-by-default when a workflow handles non-public listing drafts.
