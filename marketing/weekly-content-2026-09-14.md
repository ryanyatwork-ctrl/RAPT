## 3. SEO blog outline

**Working title:** How to Set Dynamic Pricing for Your Airbnb (Without Watching It All Day)
**Primary keyword:** `airbnb dynamic pricing` (secondary: *short term rental pricing strategy*, *airbnb pricing tool*, *how to price airbnb listing*)
**Search intent:** Informational → commercial investigation. Host knows flat pricing is costing them and wants a repeatable strategy; comparing whether to do it manually vs. use a tool. Content should teach the strategy and position RAPT as the forward-looking option, without hard-selling.
**Target length:** 1,600–2,200 words. **Meta description:** *Flat nightly rates quietly cost hosts thousands a year. Here's a practical dynamic pricing strategy for Airbnb — and how to price for demand before your booking pace reacts.*

**Outline:**

- **H1:** How to Set Dynamic Pricing for Your Airbnb (Without Watching It All Day)
  - Intro: the flat-rate tax — a quick, concrete "one weekend you gave away" example. State the promise: a strategy, not just a tool pitch.
- **H2: What dynamic pricing actually means for short-term rentals**
  - Nightly rates that move with demand; not surge-gouging. Base rate, floors/ceilings, seasonality, day-of-week.
- **H2: Why flat pricing quietly costs you the most money**
  - The two failure modes: underpriced peak nights + empty ordinary nights. Rough math on annual leakage.
- **H2: The problem with reacting to booking pace**
  - Pace is a *lagging* signal — by the time it moves, the market has repriced. Introduce forward vs. reactive pricing.
- **H2: Forward demand: pricing for what's about to happen**
  - Events, tournaments, graduations, conferences, seasonal drivers. Property-specific relevance (drive time, magnitude, lodging propensity). *Price what's about to happen, not only what already happened.*
- **H2: A simple dynamic pricing strategy you can run this week**
  - H3: Set your base rate and floor/ceiling
  - H3: Map the demand calendar around your property
  - H3: Layer day-of-week and seasonality
  - H3: Adjust for lead time and pace as confirmation
- **H2: Manual vs. rule-based vs. forward-looking tools**
  - Comparison table: effort, reaction speed, explainability. Where a tool like RAPT fits (forward event intelligence + explainable recs).
- **H2: Don't forget the listing itself**
  - Pricing fills the calendar only if the listing converts. Brief nod to titles/first-lines and HostCopy (hostcopy.gorapt.com).
- **H2: FAQ** (schema-friendly)
  - Does dynamic pricing hurt reviews? How often should rates change? Do I still set a minimum? Is it worth it for a single listing?
- **CTA:** Try forward-looking pricing on your own calendar → getrapt.app
