How GoRAPT works

GoRAPT is the public home for a small toolkit for short-term rental hosts. Each domain has one job, one primary next step, and clear links back to support and legal information.

gorapt.com is the public hub

This site explains the product network, provides trust and legal context, and routes visitors to the right tool. If someone is new to RAPT or wants support, this is the clearest starting point.

Contact GoRAPT

getrapt.app is the pricing workspace

RAPT helps hosts work through demand-aware pricing, event intelligence, calendars, revenue forecasts, and listing optimization. It is the action layer for hosts who are ready to manage properties.

Open RAPT

hostcopy.gorapt.com is the copy workflow

HostCopy turns verified property facts into listing copy for Airbnb, VRBO, RAPT, and direct-booking pages. It stays focused on copy output rather than competing with the main pricing app.
